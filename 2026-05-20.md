### [Jury Rejects All of Elon Musk's Claims Against OpenAI and Sam Altman](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/18/nx-s1-5822366/musk-altman-openai-jury-verdict-claims-dismissed)
**Source:** NPR | **Published:** May 18, 2026
**Summary:** An Oakland federal jury dismissed every claim in Elon Musk's lawsuit against Sam Altman and OpenAI, finding that Musk's "breach of charitable trust" allegations were filed outside the three-year statute of limitations. The advisory verdict — delivered after less than two hours of deliberation — was immediately adopted by District Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers. Musk called the outcome a "calendar technicality" on X and vowed to appeal.
**Why it matters:** The verdict removes a significant legal cloud over OpenAI's planned IPO and its conversion from a nonprofit to a for-profit public benefit corporation.

### [NVIDIA Ising: World's First Open-Source Quantum AI Model Family Released](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-launches-ising-the-worlds-first-open-ai-models-to-accelerate-the-path-to-useful-quantum-computers)
**Source:** NVIDIA Newsroom | **Published:** April 14, 2026
**Summary:** NVIDIA released Ising, the first family of open-source AI models designed specifically for quantum computing infrastructure. The family includes Ising Calibration (a 35B-parameter vision-language model that automates qubit measurement and hardware retuning, cutting calibration time from days to hours) and Ising Decoding (3D neural networks for real-time error correction, 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate than traditional decoders). Early adopters include Fermilab, Harvard SEAS, IQM Quantum Computers, and the UK National Physical Laboratory.
**Why it matters:** AI-driven quantum calibration and error correction could dramatically accelerate the timeline to fault-tolerant quantum computing by removing its most labor-intensive bottleneck.
