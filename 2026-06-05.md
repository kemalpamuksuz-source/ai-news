### [MiniMax Launches M3: Open-Weight Model with 1M Context, Frontier Coding, and Native Multimodality](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-m3)
**Source:** MiniMax / The Decoder | **Published:** June 1, 2026
**Summary:** Shanghai-based MiniMax released M3, an open-weight model powered by its proprietary MiniMax Sparse Attention (MSA) architecture that reduces per-token compute to 1/20th of previous-generation models at a 1M-token context length. M3 scores 59.0% on SWE-Bench Pro — surpassing GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro — and leads on BrowseComp autonomous web search with 83.5 points. Weights are being published to Hugging Face and GitHub within ten days of launch, though training code is not yet released.
**Why it matters:** M3 is the first open-weight model combining 1M-token context, native multimodality, and frontier-tier coding in a single system, raising the ceiling for the open ecosystem.

### [EU AI Act Omnibus Agreement: Major Simplification and New Prohibitions Reached](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/)
**Source:** Council of the EU / Global Policy Watch | **Published:** May 7, 2026
**Summary:** EU institutions reached a provisional political agreement on the "AI Omnibus" — the first set of amendments to the AI Act since its 2024 adoption. Key changes include a 16-month compliance extension for high-risk AI systems (now due December 2027), expanded access to regulatory sandboxes for smaller companies, centralized oversight of general-purpose AI models under the AI Office, and two new prohibitions covering non-consensual intimate image generation and CSAM. Formal adoption and publication in the Official Journal is expected this summer.
**Why it matters:** These amendments substantially reshape compliance timelines for thousands of companies and clarify the governance framework as the August 2026 full-applicability date approaches.
