### [Anthropic Confidentially Files for IPO at ~$965B Valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-files-to-go-public/)
**Source:** TechCrunch | **Published:** June 1, 2026
**Summary:** Anthropic submitted a confidential draft S-1 registration statement to the SEC on June 1, formally beginning the IPO process. The filing came days after the company closed a $65B Series H round that pushed its valuation to $965B — ahead of OpenAI's last private round of $852B. Annualized revenue is approaching $47B, with the company expected to reach its first operating profit in Q2 2026.
**Why it matters:** Anthropic going public would be one of the largest tech IPOs ever and would mark a major inflection point for the AI industry's transition from venture-funded to public-market scrutiny.

### [DeepSeek Eyes $7.4B Funding Round in Historic First External Raise](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/deepseek-eyes-7-4-billion-123039218.html)

### [Trump Signs AI Executive Order Seeking Voluntary 30-Day Model Review](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/02/nx-s1-5844347/ai-safety-trump-executive-order)
**Source:** NPR | **Published:** June 2, 2026
**Summary:** President Trump signed "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security," an executive order asking major AI companies to voluntarily submit frontier models to the government for up to 30 days before public release. The order also directs agencies to build AI cybersecurity benchmarks and establish an "AI cybersecurity clearinghouse." An earlier draft mandated a 90-day review but was scrapped after industry pushback.
**Why it matters:** While voluntary, this sets a precedent for government access to pre-release frontier models and marks the Trump administration's first concrete AI safety mechanism.

### [Bipartisan "Great American AI Act" Draft Proposes First Federal AI Framework](https://rollcall.com/2026/06/04/bipartisan-ai-draft-proposes-three-year-preemption-of-state-laws/)
**Source:** Roll Call | **Published:** June 4, 2026
**Summary:** Representatives Jay Obernolte (R-CA) and Lori Trahan (D-MA) released a discussion draft of the Great American AI Act, the first bipartisan attempt at comprehensive federal AI governance. Key provisions include requiring large frontier developers (>$500M revenue) to publish AI risk frameworks, and a three-year preemption of state-level AI development regulations. The draft invites public feedback before formal introduction.
**Why it matters:** If enacted, the GAAIA would be the first U.S. federal law specifically governing frontier AI, settling the fragmented patchwork of state laws and creating a national standard.

### [The Hands-Off Era of AI Oversight Is Ending](https://www.csmonitor.com/USA/Politics/2026/0610/artificial-intelligence-regulation-trump-congress)
**Source:** Christian Science Monitor | **Published:** June 10, 2026
**Summary:** An in-depth analysis published June 10 tracks how the combination of Trump's AI executive order, the GAAIA draft, and Colorado's AI Act (effective June 30) mark a definitive end to the regulatory vacuum that characterized AI's first decade. Both parties are now converging on the view that some federal AI governance is unavoidable, though they differ sharply on scope.
**Why it matters:** The emerging bipartisan consensus on federal AI oversight represents a structural shift in how the U.S. government relates to AI development — with lasting consequences for labs, startups, and deployers alike.

### [MiniMax M3: Open-Weight Model Combines Frontier Coding, 1M Context, and Native Multimodality](https://the-decoder.com/minimax-m3-open-weight-model-with-a-million-token-context-challenges-proprietary-leaders/)
**Source:** The Decoder | **Published:** June 1, 2026
**Summary:** MiniMax launched M3, billed as the first open-weights model to combine frontier-tier coding performance, a one-million-token context window (5× its predecessor), and native multimodal computer use. M3 scored 59.0% on SWE-Bench Pro — above GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro — while per-token compute at 1M context is just 1/20th of previous-generation systems. Weights are being released on Hugging Face within ~10 days of launch.
**Why it matters:** M3 raises the ceiling for what open-weight models can do, putting pressure on closed-source providers and advancing the case that frontier capability no longer requires proprietary access.
