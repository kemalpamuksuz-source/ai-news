## 1. Model Releases & Updates

### [Experts Say Exploiting Anthropic's Fable Isn't How Kimi K3 Got So Good](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/experts-say-exploiting-anthropics-fable-isnt-how-kimi-k3-got-so-good/)
**Source:** TechCrunch | **Published:** July 23, 2026
**Summary:** White House science advisor Michael Kratsios accused China's Moonshot AI of covertly distilling Anthropic's Fable model to build Kimi K3, its 2.8-trillion-parameter open-weight model — the largest such model ever released. However, AI researchers and experts pushed back, arguing that distillation alone from a model available publicly only since July 1 cannot account for Kimi K3's frontier-class performance across coding, reasoning, and long-context tasks.
**Why it matters:** The dispute highlights that Chinese open-weight models have reached performance parity with top US closed models, intensifying the geopolitical stakes of open-weight AI development.

## 2. Research Breakthroughs

### [How AI Helps Scientists Design the Next Generation of Medicines](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/07/23/1140346/how-ai-helps-scientists-design-the-next-generation-of-medicines/)
**Source:** MIT Technology Review | **Published:** July 23, 2026
**Summary:** AI systems are now generating and prioritizing candidate drug molecules computationally, predicting which molecular designs are most likely to succeed before any physical experiment is run. This allows researchers to redirect wet-lab resources to only the top-ranked candidates, compressing the earliest stages of drug discovery from years to months in some cases.
**Why it matters:** AI-driven molecular design is shifting drug discovery from a trial-and-error process to a data-driven pipeline, with the potential to radically cut the cost and timeline of bringing new medicines to patients.

## 5. Practical AI Tools

### [OpenAI Makes ChatGPT Health Available to All US Users](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/openai-makes-chatgpt-health-available-to-all-u-s-users/)
**Source:** TechCrunch | **Published:** July 23, 2026
**Summary:** OpenAI opened ChatGPT Health to all US users aged 18 and older across Free, Go, Plus, and Pro plans. The feature connects to Apple Health, Epic and Oracle Health medical records, One Medical, and Function Health, enabling ChatGPT to compare lab results, summarize changes since prior appointments, track medications, and correlate activity and sleep data with health outcomes. OpenAI confirmed that connected health data is never used to train its foundation models or target advertising.
**Why it matters:** Deploying health record integration at ChatGPT's scale — over 300 million weekly active users — represents the largest-ever rollout of AI-assisted personal health analysis to the general public, raising both the potential for benefit and the scrutiny on data practices.
