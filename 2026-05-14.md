### [Palo Alto Warns AI-Driven Cyberattacks Will Be the "New Norm" Within Months](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/13/palo-alto-ai-cyberattacks-mythos-gpt.html)
**Source:** CNBC | **Published:** May 13, 2026
**Summary:** Palo Alto Networks CTO Lee Klarich warned that organizations have a three-to-five-month window before AI-powered exploits become routine. The company's own red team, using Anthropic's restricted Mythos model and OpenAI's GPT-5.5-Cyber, discovered 85 vulnerabilities in weeks — prompting Palo Alto to release 26 CVEs in a single May advisory, compared to the usual fewer than five. The models demonstrated "intuitive understanding" of software flaws and chained them autonomously.
**Why it matters:** This is the clearest industry evidence yet that frontier AI has crossed the threshold from security assistant to autonomous offensive agent, fundamentally compressing the window defenders have to act.

### [EU Council and Parliament Agree to Simplify AI Act Rules](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/)
**Source:** Council of the EU | **Published:** May 7, 2026
**Summary:** The EU Council and Parliament reached a political agreement on the "AI omnibus" package, a set of targeted amendments to the AI Act designed to reduce compliance burdens before high-risk AI provisions enter force on August 2, 2026. Key changes include extending regulatory exemptions to small mid-caps, reducing the transparency grace period for AI-generated content from six to three months (new deadline: December 2, 2026), and postponing mandatory AI sandboxes to August 2027. The AI Office's enforcement powers are also reinforced.
**Why it matters:** With full AI Act applicability eight weeks away, this agreement sets the final compliance baseline for every company deploying AI systems in the EU.

**Source:** CNBC | **Published:** May 11, 2026
