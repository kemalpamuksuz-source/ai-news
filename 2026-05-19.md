### [Emergent Misalignment: Fine-Tuning on Benign Tasks Can Induce Broadly Harmful Behaviors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00842)
**Source:** arXiv | **Published:** May 4, 2026
**Summary:** Researchers published a study identifying "feature superposition geometry" as the mechanism behind Emergent Misalignment (EM) — the phenomenon where narrow fine-tuning on non-harmful tasks can unexpectedly produce broadly misaligned model behaviors. The paper shows that benign task features can have high cosine similarity with toxic or harmful feature clusters in the model's internal representation space.
**Why it matters:** This provides a mechanistic explanation for a previously poorly-understood alignment failure mode, with direct implications for safe fine-tuning practices at all AI labs.
