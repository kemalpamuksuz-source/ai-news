### [Google Will Pay SpaceX $920M/Month in $30 Billion AI Compute Deal](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/05/google-will-pay-spacex-920m-per-month-for-compute/)
**Source:** TechCrunch | **Published:** June 5, 2026

### [Trump Signs Executive Order on AI Innovation and Security](https://www.insideprivacy.com/artificial-intelligence/white-house-releases-executive-order-on-advanced-ai-innovation-and-security/)
**Source:** Inside Privacy | **Published:** June 2, 2026
**Summary:** The White House issued an executive order directing formation of an AI Cybersecurity Clearinghouse within 30 days and creating a voluntary framework under which developers provide the government early access to frontier models for up to 30 days before release. The order also instructs federal agencies to modernize AI-dependent systems and explicitly bars creation of mandatory pre-clearance or licensing requirements. A companion directive instructs the White House to challenge state AI laws deemed to inhibit innovation.
**Why it matters:** The EO sets the de facto U.S. federal AI governance template and directly pits federal preemption against the patchwork of state laws like Colorado's and California's that are already in force.

### [NVIDIA Releases Nemotron 3 Ultra: 550B Open-Weight Hybrid Model](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/06/04/nvidia-ai-releases-nemotron-3-ultra-an-open-550b-mixture-of-experts-hybrid-mamba-transformer-for-long-running-agents/)
**Source:** MarkTechPost | **Published:** June 4, 2026
**Summary:** NVIDIA shipped Nemotron 3 Ultra, a 550B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model (55B active per pass) built on a hybrid Mamba-Transformer architecture optimized for long-running agents with 200K+ token contexts. It posts the highest Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score (48) of any U.S.-developed open-weight model, delivers 300+ tokens per second on NVIDIA hardware, and weights are freely available on Hugging Face. The model was first announced at Computex 2026 on June 1.
**Why it matters:** A top-tier open-weight model from NVIDIA — with its hardware ecosystem advantage — puts serious pressure on closed-API providers and democratizes frontier agent capabilities.
