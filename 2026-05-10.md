### [AI Co-Mathematician: Accelerating Mathematicians with Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06651)
**Source:** arXiv / Google DeepMind | **Published:** May 7, 2026
**Summary:** Google DeepMind published a paper introducing an AI Co-Mathematician — a stateful, multi-agent system built on Gemini that collaborates with human researchers on open-ended mathematical problems. The system scored 48% on FrontierMath Tier 4 (23 of 48 non-public problems), a new benchmark record, outperforming GPT-5.5 Pro at 39.6%. In a real-world test, it helped Oxford mathematician Marc Lackenby resolve an open problem from the Kourovka Notebook (group theory, open for 60+ years).
**Why it matters:** Scoring nearly half of FrontierMath Tier 4 — previously considered years away from AI reach — signals that autonomous AI assistance on frontier mathematics research is arriving now.

### [DeepSeek's mHC Paper Called a "Striking Breakthrough" in AI Model Scaling](https://deepseek.ai/blog/deepseek-mhc-manifold-constrained-hyper-connections)
**Source:** DeepSeek AI Blog / arXiv | **Published:** 2026
**Summary:** DeepSeek's Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections (mHC) paper proposes a replacement for standard residual connections in transformers that enforces information conservation across layers, preventing the signal explosion (3000× amplification in 27B-param models) that makes very deep networks unstable. The method adds only ~6–7% training overhead and enables smooth scaling to sizes where unconstrained architectures previously diverged.
**Why it matters:** If mHC generalizes across architectures, it could remove one of the key engineering barriers to training significantly larger and deeper models cost-effectively.
