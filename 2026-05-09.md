### [Trump Administration Reverses Course on AI Oversight, Eyes Mandatory Pre-Release Model Vetting](https://fortune.com/2026/05/06/trump-administration-embraces-ai-oversight-policies-it-once-rejected-anthropic-mythos-caisi/)
**Source:** Fortune / The Washington Post | **Published:** May 6–8, 2026
**Summary:** In a dramatic policy reversal, the Trump White House is now weighing an executive order that would give the federal government a formal role in vetting AI models before public release — a position the administration had previously rejected. The pivot was triggered by Anthropic's Mythos model, a cybersecurity-focused AI capable of identifying and exploiting software vulnerabilities at scale; Anthropic voluntarily limited its rollout under a program called Project Glasswing. The Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI) has already signed agreements with Google DeepMind, Microsoft, and xAI to allow government evaluation of their models prior to release.
**Why it matters:** This marks the first serious federal move toward pre-release AI regulation in the US, potentially reshaping how frontier labs ship new models.

### [OpenAI Releases GPT-5.5 Instant as ChatGPT's New Default Model](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/05/openai-releases-gpt-5-5-instant-a-new-default-model-for-chatgpt/)
**Source:** TechCrunch / OpenAI | **Published:** May 5, 2026
**Summary:** OpenAI updated ChatGPT's default model to GPT-5.5 Instant, replacing GPT-5.3 Instant and rolling out to all users worldwide. The updated model produces 52.5% fewer hallucinated claims on high-stakes prompts in medicine, law, and finance compared to its predecessor, and delivers more concise, better-calibrated answers. GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.5 Pro (a parallel-compute variant) are also available via API.
**Why it matters:** With 910 million weekly active users, a default model swap of this magnitude affects more people than any previous OpenAI release.

### [Kimi K2.6: The First Open-Weights Model to Seriously Challenge GPT-5.5 on Coding](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/kimi-k2-6-the-new-leading-open-weights-model)
**Source:** Artificial Analysis / Hugging Face | **Published:** April–May 2026
**Summary:** Moonshot AI's Kimi K2.6 (1T total parameters, 32B active, MoE architecture) scored 80.2% on SWE-Bench Verified and 58.6% on SWE-Bench Pro — placing it at or above GPT-5.5 on software engineering benchmarks while remaining fully open-weight under a Modified MIT license.
