### [Colorado AI Act Overhauled — Enforcement Now Pushed to January 2027](https://www.alstonprivacy.com/compliance-deadline-for-colorado-ai-act-delayed-until-june-30-2026/)
**Source:** Alston & Bird Privacy Blog | **Published:** May 2026
**Summary:** Colorado Governor Polis signed SB 189 on May 14, 2026, substantially rewriting the original Colorado AI Act and pushing the effective date from June 30, 2026, to January 1, 2027. The revised law abandons the original risk-based duty-of-care requirements — algorithmic-discrimination prevention, impact assessments, AG reporting — in favor of a narrower transparency-and-disclosure framework. A federal stay had already halted enforcement of the original version in April.
**Why it matters:** Colorado was the bellwether for comprehensive U.S. state AI legislation; its retreat to a lighter-touch approach may slow momentum for similar laws in other states and hand advantage to the federal preemption push.

### [NVIDIA Open-Sources Ising: World's First AI Models for Quantum Computing](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-launches-ising-the-worlds-first-open-ai-models-to-accelerate-the-path-to-useful-quantum-computers)
**Source:** NVIDIA Newsroom | **Published:** April 14, 2026
**Summary:** NVIDIA released Ising, a family of open-source AI models tackling quantum computing's two biggest bottlenecks: processor calibration and real-time error correction. The models deliver 2.5× faster and 3× more accurate error-correction decoding, and reduce calibration time from days to hours. Early adopters include IonQ, Fermilab, Harvard, and the UK's National Physical Laboratory.
**Why it matters:** By positioning AI as the "control plane" of quantum machines and open-sourcing the framework, NVIDIA is making a strategic bid to own the infrastructure layer of the quantum era before it arrives.

### [Microsoft Open-Sources RAMPART and Clarity for AI Agent Safety in CI/CD Pipelines](https://campustechnology.com/articles/2026/05/20/microsoft-releases-open-source-ai-safety-tools-for-agent-development.aspx)
**Source:** Campus Technology | **Published:** May 20, 2026
**Summary:** Microsoft released two open-source AI safety tools: RAMPART (a Pytest-native red-teaming framework for testing AI agents against prompt injection, behavioral regressions, and data exfiltration); and Clarity (a design-validation tool that checks agent architecture assumptions before implementation begins). ### [Anthropic Splits Claude Subscriptions: Agent Usage Gets Separate Credit Pool from June 15](https://www.infoworld.com/article/4171274/anthropic-puts-claude-agents-on-a-meter-across-its-subscriptions.html)
**Source:** InfoWorld | **Published:** May 2026
**Summary:** Starting June 15, 2026, Anthropic will separate programmatic agent usage (Claude Agent SDK, non-interactive Claude Code, third-party agents) from interactive chat into distinct monthly credit pools — $20/month for Pro, $100–$200 for Max tiers — metered at full API rates with no rollover. ### [White House Unveils National AI Legislative Framework to Preempt State AI Laws](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/03/president-donald-j-trump-unveils-national-ai-legislative-framework/)
**Source:** The White House | **Published:** March 2026
**Summary:** The Trump Administration released a comprehensive national AI legislative framework in March 2026 aimed at centralizing AI regulation at the federal level and preempting the growing patchwork of state AI laws. The framework preserves state authority over child safety, consumer fraud, and zoning, but would override state algorithmic-discrimination, transparency, and AI-accountability statutes. It also proposes a federal "safe harbor" for developers who comply with national standards.
**Why it matters:** Federal preemption of state AI laws would dramatically simplify compliance for AI companies while potentially undercutting protections in states that have moved faster than Congress.
