### [China Blocks Meta's $2 Billion Acquisition of AI Startup Manus](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-27/china-blocks-meta-s-2-billion-acquisition-of-ai-startup-manus)
**Source:** Bloomberg | **Published:** April 27, 2026
**Summary:** China's National Development and Reform Commission ordered Meta to fully unwind its acquisition of Manus, a Chinese-founded agentic AI startup, citing national security and technology export concerns. ### [Google Signs $200M Pentagon AI Contract After Anthropic's Refusal](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/28/google-expands-pentagons-access-to-its-ai-after-anthropics-refusal/)
**Source:** TechCrunch | **Published:** April 28, 2026
**Summary:** Google signed a $200 million agreement giving the Pentagon access to its AI for "any lawful purpose," with carve-outs against lethal autonomous weapons and domestic surveillance without human oversight. The deal follows Anthropic's refusal to strip safety guardrails for the DoD — a stand that led the Pentagon to brand Anthropic a "supply-chain risk," triggering ongoing litigation. OpenAI and xAI also signed similar deals earlier.
**Why it matters:** The contrasting stances of Google and Anthropic on defense AI are crystallizing a defining industry debate about where frontier AI companies draw the line on safety versus government access.

### [DeepMind's David Silver Raises Record $1.1B Seed for Ineffable Intelligence](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/27/deepminds-david-silver-just-raised-1-1b-to-build-an-ai-that-learns-without-human-data/)
**Source:** TechCrunch | **Published:** April 27, 2026
**Summary:** David Silver — UCL professor and the Google DeepMind researcher behind AlphaGo — raised $1.1 billion in seed funding for Ineffable Intelligence, Europe's largest-ever seed round, valuing the startup at $5.1 billion. Backers include Sequoia, Lightspeed, NVIDIA, Google, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund. The company is building AI that learns from experience through reinforcement learning rather than human-labeled data.
**Why it matters:** The record-breaking round reflects surging conviction that RL-first approaches are the next step toward AGI, and cements London as a tier-one hub for frontier AI research alongside San Francisco.

### [White House National AI Policy Framework Calls to Preempt State AI Laws](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/white-house-releases-a-national-policy-framework-for-artificial)
**Source:** Holland & Knight | **Published:** March 20, 2026 (April 8 analysis)
**Summary:** The White House released a National Policy Framework for AI recommending that Congress preempt state AI laws that "impose undue burdens," establish a single federal standard, and avoid creating any new AI-specific regulatory body. The Framework also calls for child privacy protections and age-verification requirements. Congress has so far declined to enact broad federal preemption.
**Why it matters:** The tension between the Administration's preemption push and advancing state-level AI laws (Colorado, California) will define the regulatory compliance landscape for every US AI company in the next two years.
