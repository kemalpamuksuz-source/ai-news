### [NIST Publishes Independent Evaluation of DeepSeek V4 Pro](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/05/caisi-evaluation-deepseek-v4-pro)
**Source:** NIST / Center for AI Standards and Innovation | **Published:** May 2026
**Summary:** NIST's CAISI unit released its evaluation of DeepSeek V4 Pro, finding its capabilities trail leading U.S. closed models by roughly eight months — performing similarly to GPT-5 rather than the current frontier. However, on cost-efficiency, DeepSeek V4 outperformed GPT-5.4 mini on five of seven benchmarks, and it is the most capable PRC model CAISI has evaluated across cyber, software engineering, math, and abstract reasoning domains.
**Why it matters:** The first independent U.S. government benchmark of DeepSeek's latest model gives policymakers and developers a clearer, non-self-reported picture of the China-U.S. AI capability gap.

### [Google's TurboQuant Achieves 6× KV Cache Compression with Zero Accuracy Loss](https://research.google/blog/turboquant-redefining-ai-efficiency-with-extreme-compression/)
**Source:** Google Research | **Published:** ICLR 2026
**Summary:** Google DeepMind presented TurboQuant at ICLR 2026, a two-stage algorithm (PolarQuant rotation + sub-3-bit quantization) that compresses LLM key-value caches by 6× with no degradation on standard benchmarks. It requires no fine-tuning or calibration data and outperforms JAX baselines in raw attention compute speed. Community implementations for llama.cpp and PyTorch have already appeared on GitHub.
**Why it matters:** KV cache memory is a primary bottleneck for long-context inference; a training-free 6× reduction could meaningfully cut the cost of running million-token-context models in production.
