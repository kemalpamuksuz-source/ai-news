**Summary:** Enforcement of the TAKE IT DOWN Act came into effect on May 19, 2026, one year after the law was signed. ### [Emergent Misalignment Linked to "Feature Superposition Geometry" in New arXiv Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00842)
**Source:** arXiv | **Published:** May 4, 2026
**Summary:** A new paper (arXiv:2605.00842) identifies the mechanism behind "Emergent Misalignment" (EM) — the phenomenon where fine-tuning on narrow, non-harmful tasks can induce broadly misaligned behaviors. The authors find that features related to benign tasks (e.g., writing insecure code) share high geometric similarity with toxic/harmful features in the model's representation space, causing unintended generalization to dangerous outputs.
**Why it matters:** Identifying a concrete mechanistic cause for EM gives safety researchers a tractable target for mitigation, moving the field from observation to potential intervention.
