### [Pope Leo XIV Publishes "Magnifica Humanitas" — First Papal Encyclical on Artificial Intelligence](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2026-05/pope-leo-xiv-first-encyclical-magnifica-humanitas.html)
**Source:** Vatican News | **Published:** May 25, 2026
**Summary:** Pope Leo XIV released his first encyclical letter, *Magnifica Humanitas* ("Magnificent Humanity"), at 11:30 a.m. at the Vatican's Synod Hall, presenting it alongside Anthropic co-founder Christopher Olah. Signed on May 15 — exactly 135 years after Leo XIII signed *Rerum Novarum* — the document centers on "the protection of the human person in the age of artificial intelligence" and frames AI as a moral challenge comparable to industrialization. Olah participated in the panel presentation, marking an unprecedented collaboration between a Catholic head of state and a frontier AI lab.
**Why it matters:** A papal encyclical carries formal doctrinal weight for 1.4 billion Catholics and tends to shape broader global policy discourse — this is the most significant moral-authority statement on AI ethics to date.

### [TAKE IT DOWN Act Takes Effect: FTC Enforces Deepfake Removal Law, First Arrests Made](https://www.justice.gov/usao-edny/pr/two-individuals-arrested-publishing-ai-deepfake-pornography-violation-take-it-down-act)
**Source:** U.S. Department of Justice | **Published:** May 2026
**Summary:** The TAKE IT DOWN Act's platform-enforcement provisions came into force on May 19, 2026, requiring covered platforms to remove non-consensual intimate imagery — including AI-generated deepfakes — within 48 hours of a takedown request. The DOJ's Eastern District of New York announced the first criminal arrests under the law in May, charging two individuals with publishing AI deepfake pornography; a prior Ohio conviction in April 2026 involved AI-generated CSAM. The FTC is now responsible for ongoing civil enforcement.
**Why it matters:** This marks the first federal AI-specific criminal enforcement in the United States, setting a direct legal precedent for how AI-generated harmful content will be prosecuted going forward.

**Source:** CNBC | **Published:** May 21, 2026
**Summary:** Anthropic is in early talks with Microsoft to deploy its Maia 200 custom AI silicon — a chip Microsoft has developed but not yet released to Azure customers — for training and inference workloads. **Why it matters:** A signed deal would validate Microsoft's custom silicon strategy, reduce Anthropic's dependency on Nvidia GPUs, and intensify the competition among cloud providers for frontier-lab compute contracts.

### [Microsoft Open-Sources RAMPART and Clarity to Harden AI Agent Safety Engineering](https://www.csoonline.com/article/4175592/microsoft-releases-open-source-tools-to-operationalize-ai-agent-safety-2.html)
**Source:** CSO Online | **Published:** May 20, 2026

**Source:** Search Engine Journal | **Published:** May 2026

### [Over 200,000 MCP Servers Found Vulnerable to Remote Command Execution](https://www.xloggs.com/2026/05/25/weekly-threat-report-2026-05-25/)
**Source:** Xloggs AI Security | **Published:** May 25, 2026
**Summary:** Security researchers scanning the public internet identified more than 200,000 servers running Model Context Protocol (MCP) — the standard that allows AI agents to call external tools and APIs — with exploitable command execution flaws, including unauthenticated remote execution paths. The same analysis found that 88% of 2.7 million arXiv submissions inadvertently contained material not intended for public release, raising separate concerns about training data provenance and accidental information leakage.
**Why it matters:** MCP is rapidly becoming the connective tissue of the agentic AI ecosystem; mass-scale remote code execution vulnerabilities in this layer represent a systemic risk to any enterprise running AI agents in production.
