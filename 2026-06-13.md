### 3. [Visa Embeds Its Payment Network Into ChatGPT, Enabling AI Agents to Shop Autonomously](https://fortune.com/2026/06/11/visa-chatgpt-ai-agent-payments-shopping/)
**Source:** Fortune / ABC News | **Published:** June 11–12, 2026
**Summary:** At the Visa Payments Forum in San Francisco, Visa announced that its payment network is now embedded inside ChatGPT, allowing AI agents to independently complete purchases at any Visa-accepting merchant on users' behalf. **Why it matters:** This moves AI agents from recommending purchases to completing them autonomously at global scale, opening a new category of agentic commerce with immediate reach across Visa's worldwide merchant network.

### 5. [NVIDIA Launches Cosmos 3: The First Fully Open Omnimodel for Physical AI](https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/06/01/nvidia-launches-cosmos-3-the-open-frontier-foundation-model-for-physical-ai/)
**Source:** HPCwire / Axios | **Published:** June 1, 2026
**Summary:** NVIDIA released Cosmos 3, the world's first fully open omnimodel, unifying language, image, video, audio, and action generation in a single architecture trained for physical accuracy — enabling robots and autonomous vehicles to simulate real-world environments with correct physics. Released under the OpenMDW-1.1 License with weights, code, datasets, and fine-tuning recipes, NVIDIA also launched the Cosmos Coalition with Runway and other partners to build an open ecosystem around world models.
**Why it matters:** Open access to a physically-accurate omnimodel has the potential to compress physical AI training cycles from months to days and democratizes capabilities that previously required proprietary lab infrastructure.
