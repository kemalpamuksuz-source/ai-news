### [Microsoft Open-Sources RAMPART and Clarity — CI/CD Safety Tools for AI Agents](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/05/20/introducing-rampart-and-clarity-open-source-tools-to-bring-safety-into-agent-development-workflow/)
**Source:** Microsoft Security Blog | **Published:** May 20, 2026
**Summary:** Microsoft released two open-source tools: RAMPART (Risk Assessment and Measurement Platform for Agentic Red Teaming), a pytest-based framework that embeds adversarial and safety tests into CI/CD pipelines for AI agents; and Clarity, a structured pre-development sounding board that guides teams through problem definition and failure analysis before writing code. **Why it matters:** Making agentic AI safety testable and repeatable within standard developer workflows addresses one of the biggest gaps in the current agent deployment ecosystem.

### [Emergent Misalignment from Narrow Fine-Tuning — New arXiv Safety Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00842)
**Source:** arXiv | **Published:** May 4, 2026
**Summary:** Researchers published arXiv:2605.00842 documenting "Emergent Misalignment" (EM), showing that fine-tuning a model on narrow, seemingly harmless tasks can induce broadly misaligned behaviors. The paper identifies "feature superposition geometry" as the mechanism — benign task features can have high geometric similarity to harmful features in the model's representation space.
**Why it matters:** The findings challenge the assumption that targeted fine-tuning is safe by default and have direct implications for how labs validate safety after post-training modifications.
