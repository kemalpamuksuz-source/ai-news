### [Google Detects First AI-Generated Zero-Day Exploit Used in the Wild](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-11/hackers-used-ai-to-build-zero-day-attack-google-researchers-say)
**Source:** Bloomberg / CNBC / SecurityWeek | **Published:** May 11, 2026
**Summary:** Google's Threat Intelligence Group reported that it thwarted a criminal hacker group's attempt to use an AI model to find and exploit a zero-day vulnerability — specifically crafting a bypass for two-factor authentication — in what researchers are calling the first confirmed AI-generated zero-day exploit used in a real cyberattack. Google says it has "high confidence" the attackers planned a mass vulnerability exploitation operation, and its proactive counter-discovery may have prevented it. The tools used were commercially available AI (not Google's own Gemini), demonstrating that offensive AI-assisted hacking has crossed from theoretical to operational.
**Why it matters:** This is a watershed moment for cybersecurity — AI-assisted zero-day discovery at the hands of criminal actors signals a step-change in the threat landscape that defenders must now plan around.

### [EU Council and Parliament Agree to Streamline AI Act Rules](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/)
**Source:** Council of the EU (official) | **Published:** May 7, 2026
**Summary:** The European Council and Parliament reached a political agreement to simplify key provisions of the EU AI Act ahead of its full applicability on August 2, 2026. Changes extend regulatory exemptions for SMEs to small mid-caps, shorten grace periods for AI-generated content transparency from six to three months (deadline: December 2, 2026), and push AI sandbox deployment deadlines to 2027. The Commission simultaneously opened a consultation on draft transparency obligation guidelines.
**Why it matters:** With high-risk AI rules coming into force in August, this agreement sets the final compliance landscape for thousands of companies operating in or selling into Europe.
