### [EU Reaches Political Agreement on AI Act Omnibus—Extends Key Deadlines](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/)
**Source:** Council of the EU | **Published:** May 7, 2026
**Summary:** The European Council, Parliament, and Commission reached a provisional political agreement on the "Digital Omnibus on AI," simplifying high-risk AI system obligations and extending the August 2, 2026 compliance deadline for use-based Annex III systems by 16 months to December 2, 2027. Synthetic-content marking obligations are also pushed from August 2026 to December 2026. Formal adoption is expected by July 2026.
**Why it matters:** The deadline relief gives companies—especially U.S. operators—significantly more runway to build compliant workflows without regulatory penalty.
