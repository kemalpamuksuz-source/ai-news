### [Google's TurboQuant Achieves 6× KV Cache Compression With Zero Accuracy Loss](https://research.google/blog/turboquant-redefining-ai-efficiency-with-extreme-compression/)
**Source:** Google Research Blog | **Published:** April 25, 2026 (ICLR 2026)
**Summary:** Google Research presented TurboQuant at ICLR 2026, an inference-time algorithm that compresses LLM key-value caches to 3 bits per element — a 6× reduction — with no measurable accuracy degradation, no retraining, and no calibration data. It combines PolarQuant vector rotation with 1-bit QJL residual correction, achieving up to 8× faster attention on NVIDIA H100 GPUs.
**Why it matters:** Dramatic KV-cache compression at inference time dramatically lowers the GPU memory cost of running long-context models, making extended-context and multi-session AI agents far more economical to operate.
