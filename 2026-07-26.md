## Model Releases & Updates

### [Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/anthropic-launches-opus-5/)
**Source:** TechCrunch | **Published:** July 24, 2026
**Summary:** Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, its new flagship model priced at $5/M input and $25/M output tokens — half the cost of Claude Fable 5 — yet outperforming Fable 5 on several benchmarks including FrontierBench v0.1 (43.3% vs. GPT-5.6 Sol's 37.5%). The model ships with a 1-million-token context window, a new effort-toggle (low/medium/high) that lets users trade compute for cost, and an opt-in Automatic Fallbacks beta that routes requests hitting safety classifiers to a lighter model instead of returning an error.
**Why it matters:** Opus 5 re-establishes Anthropic's benchmark leadership at a price point that undercuts rivals, squeezing the cost-per-capability gap that had been widening in OpenAI and Google's favor.

## AI Industry & Business

### [NAVER, Nvidia and Brookfield to Expand Korea's National AI Factory Infrastructure](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/25/3333160/0/en/NAVER-NVIDIA-and-Brookfield-to-Expand-Korea-s-National-AI-Factory-Infrastructure-Buildout.html)
**Source:** GlobeNewsWire (official press release) | **Published:** July 25, 2026
**Summary:** Nvidia will invest $1 billion in NAVER to more than triple the scale of NAVER's GAK Sejong hyperscale data center in South Korea — from 55 MW to 200 MW — using the Nvidia DSX platform. Brookfield will contribute up to $9 billion as exclusive capital partner, making the project a $10 billion buildout. Bloomberg separately reported Nvidia is also expanding its accord with SK Group to over $500 billion, including memory chip purchases and SK Group's Nvidia supercomputer buys.
**Why it matters:** The deal is one of the largest single AI infrastructure commitments in Asia, signaling that sovereign AI factory buildouts — government-adjacent, nationally-scoped GPU clusters — are accelerating as a geopolitical strategy worldwide.

### [Monday.com lays off 20% of workforce, cites AI transformation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/25/the-running-list-major-tech-layoffs-in-2026-where-employers-cited-ai/)
**Source:** TechCrunch | **Published:** July 25, 2026
**Summary:** Monday.com announced it will cut roughly 600 employees — about 20% of its workforce — as part of a "restructuring plan" tied to its "AI-driven growth strategy." Co-founder Eran Zinman told staff the move was not primarily a cost-cut, but an organizational reset for an "AI-first" operating model. The company expects $45–55 million in net restructuring charges while still projecting up to 20% year-over-year revenue growth in 2026.
**Why it matters:** Monday.com is the latest in a string of SaaS companies (Amazon, Oracle, Meta, Microsoft) publicly linking headcount reductions to AI automation — a pattern that now accounts for nearly 140,000 US tech job cuts since January 2026.

## AI Policy & Regulation

### [As US weighs response to Chinese AI, 25 companies urge against broad open-weight restrictions](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/as-us-weighs-response-to-chinese-ai-industry-urges-against-broad-open-weight-restrictions/)
**Source:** TechCrunch | **Published:** July 24, 2026
**Summary:** Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta, Hugging Face, Mistral, Palantir, Mozilla, Linux Foundation, Andreessen Horowitz, and 16 other US organizations signed a joint letter urging Washington not to impose broad restrictions on open-weight AI models in response to allegations that China's Moonshot AI distilled Anthropic's Fable model to build Kimi K3. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang made his first-ever post on X to share the letter. By July 25 the signatory count had grown to 50, with OpenAI quietly joining; Amazon and Anthropic remained absent. CNBC confirmed the letter argues broad bans risk pushing innovation overseas and undermining the open-source communities that underpin US AI competitiveness.
**Why it matters:** The letter crystallizes a widening rift inside the US AI industry over whether open-weight models are a security liability or a strategic asset — a policy question that will shape export controls, chip rules, and federal AI law for years.

## Practical AI Tools

### [OpenAI's ChatGPT Voice now lets you control AI agents on the desktop](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/openais-new-voice-mode-makes-it-to-the-chatgpt-desktop-app/)
**Source:** TechCrunch | **Published:** July 24, 2026
**Summary:** OpenAI brought ChatGPT Voice to its desktop app, powered by the GPT-Live full-duplex voice model family. Users can now speak multi-step commands to control AI agents running inside ChatGPT Work or Codex, direct computer-use tasks (web browsing, app navigation), and respond to agent check-ins — all hands-free. The feature launched globally starting July 23–24, and differs from the mobile version in that it is purpose-built to take action on a computer rather than just converse.
**Why it matters:** Voice-driven agentic computer use lowers the interaction barrier for non-technical users and sets a template for always-on, ambient AI assistants that can complete complex workflows without a keyboard.
