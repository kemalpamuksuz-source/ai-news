### [White House Signs Executive Order on Advanced AI Innovation and Security](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/)
**Source:** The White House | **Published:** June 2, 2026
**Summary:** President Trump signed an executive order establishing a voluntary benchmarking framework for frontier AI models, an AI cybersecurity clearinghouse coordinated with industry, and binding directives for hardening federal civilian systems within 30 days. **Why it matters:** The EO sets the U.S. federal AI governance posture for 2026—favoring voluntary industry cooperation over mandatory controls while opening formal government access channels to frontier models.

### [MiniMax M3: First Open-Weight Model with 1M-Token Context, Frontier Coding, and Native Multimodality](https://the-decoder.com/minimax-m3-open-weight-model-with-a-million-token-context-challenges-proprietary-leaders/)
**Source:** The Decoder | **Published:** June 1, 2026
**Summary:** Chinese AI lab MiniMax released M3, the first open-weight model to combine a 1-million-token context window, native multimodal capabilities, and frontier-tier coding performance—scoring 59.0% on SWE-Bench Pro and surpassing both GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro. The model uses a novel MiniMax Sparse Attention (MSA) architecture that cuts per-token compute to roughly 1/20th of prior designs. Model weights are being open-sourced within 10 days of launch; training code is not released.
**Why it matters:** M3 significantly narrows the open vs. closed model performance gap and gives developers access to a long-context multimodal coding agent outside U.S. lab control.

### [NVIDIA Launches Cosmos 3: Open Frontier Foundation Model for Physical AI and Robotics](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-launches-cosmos-3-the-open-frontier-foundation-model-for-physical-ai)
**Source:** NVIDIA Newsroom | **Published:** June 1, 2026
**Summary:** NVIDIA released Cosmos 3, an open omni-model that unifies physical reasoning, world video generation, and robot action generation in a single two-tower mixture-of-transformers architecture. Trained on billions of multimodal samples including video, audio, and robot action trajectories, it leads PAI-Bench and RoboLab leaderboards. **Why it matters:** Cosmos 3 provides an open foundation for building physical AI systems with far less proprietary data and compute, potentially accelerating robotics the way LLMs accelerated language AI.

### [Colorado Legislature Replaces Landmark AI Act, Delays Enforcement and Softens Scope](https://www.troutmanprivacy.com/2026/05/colorado-legislature-passes-bill-to-repeal-and-replace-colorado-ai-act/)
**Source:** Troutman Privacy | **Published:** May 2026
**Summary:** Colorado Governor Polis signed SB 189, replacing the original 2024 Colorado AI Act with a narrower law focused on disclosure and transparency rather than the original risk-based framework requiring algorithmic bias audits and deployer impact assessments. The new effective date is January 1, 2027, removing broad obligations that had worried AI developers and deployers. **Why it matters:** Colorado's reversal signals that even the most ambitious U.S. state AI regulations are retreating under pressure from the tech industry and the federal government's preemption push.
